Speak your thoughts at the speed of mind. OwnVoice is the executive-grade AI voice keyboard, wireless PC studio mic, and autonomous meeting assistant built for modern professionals, writers, founders, and developers.

Whether you are replying to messages, drafting long-form documents, writing code, or recording business meetings, OwnVoice eliminates typing fatigue with whisper-accurate voice transcription and instant tone transformation.

🚀 THREE MODES IN ONE APP
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. THE NATIVE AI VOICE KEYBOARD
• Full replacement for your standard keyboard with Obsidian Linear-grade design tokens.
• Two-thumb calibrated touch targets with zero misfires or dropped strokes during rapid typing.
• Continuous hands-free voice dictation with automatic smart punctuation, capitalization, and paragraphing.
• Instant tone switching: Clean Polish, Professional, Concise, Bullet Points, or Raw Dictation.
• Quick Snippets & Custom Vocabulary: Expand custom abbreviations and acronyms instantly.

2. AIR-TYPING: WIRELESS STUDIO MIC FOR YOUR LAPTOP / PC
• Connects to your Windows PC over local Wi-Fi in under 3 seconds with zero configuration (UDP 8766 discovery).
• Turn your phone's studio microphone into a remote input device: speak on your phone and watch words type directly into your computer cursor in real time.
• Works seamlessly with VS Code, Word, Chrome, Slack, Notion, Google Docs, and coding IDEs.
• Cross-device clipboard sync: push and pull clipboard text between your phone and laptop with a single tap.

3. AUTONOMOUS MEETING STUDIO & NOTES SYNC
• Record meetings, interviews, or lectures in high-fidelity 48kHz audio.
• Automatically synthesizes spoken conversations into structured executive summaries, key decisions, and prioritized action items.
• Syncs markdown meeting minutes directly to your PC Documents folder (Documents/OwnVoice/Meetings).
• Local-first: all notes and transcripts are saved in on-device SQLite storage.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
🔒 PRIVACY-FIRST & ZERO-RETENTION ARCHITECTURE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

• Zero Ad Networks: We do not sell your personal data, track your behavior across apps, or inject advertisements.
• Bring Your Own Key (BYOK): Connect your own Google Gemini, OpenAI, Groq, or Anthropic API key. Pay wholesale prices (~$0.001 per note) with zero markups or recurring subscriptions.
• Hardware-Backed Security: Stored API keys are encrypted on-device using Android Keystore and EncryptedSharedPreferences.
• Ephemeral Audio: Voice buffers are processed in volatile memory (RAM) and immediately purged after transcription. No audio is ever stored on third-party servers.

⚙️ PERMISSIONS & TRANSPARENCY DISCLOSURE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

• Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Required for voice transcription, air-typing, and meeting recording. Only active when you initiate voice input.
• Accessibility Service: The optional Overlay Accessibility Service is used solely to detect editable input fields and paste transcribed text hands-free when using the Floating Bubble mode. It NEVER reads passwords, logs keystrokes, tracks screens, or collects personal data.
