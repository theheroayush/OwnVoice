OwnVoice is a voice keyboard for Android. Dictate into text fields, shape text with Google Gemini, capture meeting notes, or use your phone as a microphone and input companion for your PC.

VOICE KEYBOARD
• Dictate with the OwnVoice keyboard and review your transcript before using it.
• Choose a writing tone or ask Gemini to reshape text you select.
• Build custom vocabulary and snippets for names and phrases you use often.

FLOATING DICTATION
• Start dictation from the optional floating bubble.
• Optional Accessibility auto-typing can insert a transcript into the focused text field after you review and agree to its in-app disclosure. It excludes password fields. You can leave this feature disabled.

MEETING NOTES
• Start and stop a recording when you choose.
• Send audio to Google Gemini to create a transcript or notes. Results and recent history are stored on your device until you clear them.
• Get permission from meeting participants where appropriate before recording.

PHONE AND PC
• Pair with the OwnVoice desktop companion over your selected network.
• Send dictated text, keyboard input, trackpad commands or clipboard text to the paired computer when you use those features.
• Optional sync sends selected vocabulary, snippets and settings to GitHub Gist using a token you provide.

DATA AND PERMISSIONS
• Voice audio and text you ask Gemini to process are sent to Google over HTTPS. Google handles those requests under its own terms and retention practices; OwnVoice does not control Google's retention.
• Google and GitHub API credentials are stored encrypted with Android Keystore where supported. Other preferences and history are stored in app-private storage on the device.
• Accessibility is optional. Before enabling it, OwnVoice explains the active-app and focused-field access used to insert dictated text. The service does not send its accessibility observations to OwnVoice.
• PC and clipboard features send the content or commands you choose to your paired PC or configured relay. Check the destination and network before using them.
• OwnVoice does not include advertising.
